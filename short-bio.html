Short Bio

Daniel Slamanig is a Full Professor of Cryptology at the Universität der Bundeswehr München, where he heads the Quantum Safe & Advanced Cryptography (QuSAC) Lab within the Research Institute Cyber Defence (CODE). His research focuses on provably secure and practical public-key cryptography and privacy-enhancing protocols, including their foundations and real-world applications.

Before joining Universität der Bundeswehr München, he was a Senior Scientist at the AIT Austrian Institute of Technology and a Lecturer at TU Wien. Earlier, he was a Senior Researcher and Postdoctoral Researcher at Graz University of Technology. He received his PhD in Computer Science with distinction from the University of Klagenfurt in 2011. He is also one of the designers of the Picnic family of post-quantum digital signature schemes.
